Proposed business models

All business models below are proposals. None is in operation, none is priced with validated data, and none is a tax or legal benefit claim.

Target customer

Hostel messes and catering kitchens that produce surplus cooked food, and NGOs that need food donations.

Proposed payer

Donor subscription: a mess pays for coordination tools and periodic impact reports. CSR sponsorship: a company funds coordination and reporting for a named pilot zone, with its name shown only with the sponsor's agreement.

Revenue model (proposed)

Monthly donor subscription for coordination and impact reports. Optional CSR sponsorship packages for a pilot zone.

Pricing assumptions (unvalidated)

  • No price has been tested with any mess, NGO or sponsor.
  • Any subscription figure is an assumption to be validated in customer interviews before use.
  • Sponsorship pricing depends on zone size and reporting depth; none is set.
