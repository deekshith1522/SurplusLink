How a donation moves

  1. Post: a mess lists surplus food with quantity, storage, allergens and a pickup-by time.
  2. Claim: a receiver organisation claims it if the listing is still open and fits its capacity and need.
  3. Assign: a volunteer whose location, carrying capacity and availability suit the pickup accepts it.
  4. Collect: the assigned volunteer marks it collected before the pickup-by time.
  5. Deliver: the volunteer marks it delivered. Only delivered food counts in the impact figures.

SurplusLink coordinates donations and does not certify food safety. Pickup-by is a donor-provided collection deadline, not a guarantee of food safety.
